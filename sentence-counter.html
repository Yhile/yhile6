FAQ

How does the sentence counter count sentences?

It counts text that ends with a period, question mark or exclamation mark. Common abbreviations like Mr., Dr., e.g. and decimals like 3.5 are not counted as sentence endings.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Counting happens in your browser and your text never leaves your device.
