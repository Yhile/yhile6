Frequently asked questions

Which voices are available?

The available voices depend on your browser and operating system. Chrome and Edge on Windows/Mac offer the most voices, including many languages.

Is text-to-speech free with no limits?

Yes. It uses your device's built-in speech synthesis — completely free, unlimited, no signup.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The Web Speech API uses your device's speech engine. Your text never leaves your browser.

Can I change the speed of the voice?

Yes. Use the Speed slider to set any rate from 0.5x (slow) to 2x (fast).
