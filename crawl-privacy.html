Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

crawl.yhile.com ("we", "the service") is a web scraping API. This page explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, and a password if you sign up with email. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your email address from that provider. We do not receive your Google or GitHub password.
  • Usage data: your API key, your credit balance, and the URLs you ask us to scrape.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider (Cloudflare) records request logs such as time, status and IP address, which we use to keep the service running and to stop abuse.
  • Payments: if you buy credits, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

How we use it

  • To create your account, authenticate your API key, and count your credits.
  • To run the service, fix problems, and prevent abuse.
  • To contact you about your account if needed.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Scraped content

Pages you scrape are fetched on your request. Results may be cached for a few minutes to make repeat requests faster, and are not kept after that.

Who processes your data

We use Supabase (accounts and credits), Cloudflare (hosting and logs), and Stripe (payments). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing yhileemail@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
