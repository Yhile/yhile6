Frequently asked questions

Can I generate a QR code for any URL?

Yes. You can generate a QR code for any URL, plain text, email address, phone number, or Wi-Fi credentials.

Are the generated QR codes free to use commercially?

Yes. The QR codes are generated entirely in your browser and belong to you.

What size and format can I download?

You can download the QR code as a PNG image at small (128px), medium (256px), or large (512px) sizes.

Can I customize the QR code colors?

Yes. You can change the foreground (dark) and background (light) colors before generating.
