Say what nobody else would

Each question asks you to name something: a fruit, a dinosaur, a board game. Everyone answers at the same time, and the rarer your answer, the more points you get. Typos are fine, and the answer lists are checked so made-up answers don't count.

The rarer, the better

Answers are revealed one by one with how many players say them. Say apple and you get a few points, and half that if someone else said it too. Say jujube and you take 100.

Then it gets funny

Some rounds are fill-in-the-blanks. Everyone writes the funniest answer they can, then votes for their favorite. Every fifth round is a chaos round worth double points.

15 questions, one winner

After 15 questions the leaderboard shows who won, with awards like One of a Kind and Biggest NPC.

Play your way

Quick play

Jump into a public room with up to 10 players. Games start on their own once a few people are in.

Private room

Send a link to your friends. Up to 16 players, and everyone picks their own name.

Solo vs bots

No one around? Play against bots that answer like real people. Solo games are all naming rounds.

Works on any screen

Phone, tablet or computer. Open the link and play: there's nothing to install and no account to make. Questions and answers are kept school-safe.
