Homework, sorted.

A simple homework list with reminders. Type it like you'd jot it down, or import from Canvas in one click.

Add to Chrome Free. No account.

Add homework in one line

Type "ela worksheet fri 8am" and press Enter. HomeworkX fills in the subject and due date. Tests and quizzes are spotted even if you make a typo.

Skip the copying

Open your Canvas or press Import and the assignments due in the next two weeks land in your list. Your own homework is never touched.

Reminders that make sense

The evening before and an hour before, with earlier ones for tests and projects. Quiet hours keep your night free, and a badge shows what is due soon.

Make it look the way you like

More than 20 themes in one tap, light and dark, soft and bold.

Or design your own

Colors, fonts, backgrounds and more in the built-in theme editor, with a live preview. No code needed.

Private by design

Your homework stays on your computer. The extension has no account, no tracking and no ads. Nothing you type or import is sent to us or to anyone else.
